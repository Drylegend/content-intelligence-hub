How It Works

Simple on the surface, production-minded underneath

The experience stays lightweight while the backend handles scraping, storage, AI summarization, chunking, retries, and audio streaming.

  • 1. Scrape Enter a public URL or upload a research file to start a new content session.
  • 2. Summarize Generate a condensed summary that is easier to scan, save, or convert to speech.
  • 3. Listen Stream the full text or summary in the voice and playback speed you prefer.
